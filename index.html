Hello!


I’m Rashmi Ranjan Mohanty, an architect driven by a deep passion for art, architecture, design, technology, and everything that connects them.

I graduated from the College of Engineering and Technology, Bhubaneswar in 2009.

Over the years, I’ve worked on projects of varied typologies and scales, exploring how design can shape better spaces and experiences. I’m particularly interested in the intersection of architecture and technology — where traditional design thinking meets digital innovation.

I’m currently living and working in Tokyo, learning, observing, and drawing inspiration from the city’s seamless blend of tradition and modernity.
